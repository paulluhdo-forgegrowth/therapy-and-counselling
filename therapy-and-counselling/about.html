About Kim

Professional Support With a Human Approach

Kim van Zeeventer is a registered counselling psychologist based in Edenvale, offering confidential, practical support for individuals and couples — in person and online.

Registered counselling psychologist

Biography

A background in care, a practical approach

Kim works with individuals and couples, both in person and online, offering support that is confidential, practical and centred around each person she sees. Her approach is warm and non-judgemental, moving at a pace that feels manageable.

Her path into psychology began in healthcare. She trained and worked as a nurse, including in neonatal care, before completing a Master's degree in psychology at the University of Johannesburg in 2009 and registering as a psychologist in 2010.

From 2017 to 2020 she practised in Doha, Qatar, supporting clients from a wide range of backgrounds, before returning to private practice in Edenvale. She also facilitates couples workshops informed by Gottman relationship principles.

Counselling Philosophy

Counselling that feels like a genuine conversation

Kim believes support works best when it feels human. Sessions are collaborative and unhurried — a space to be heard, to make sense of what you're facing, and to find practical ways forward that fit your life.

Gottman Workshop Training

Trained to lead couples workshops

Kim is trained through The Gottman Institute to facilitate couples workshops built on decades of relationship research. These workshops give couples practical, evidence-informed tools to understand their patterns, communicate more clearly and reconnect.
