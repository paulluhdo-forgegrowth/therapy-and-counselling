Services & Contact

Counselling Services in Edenvale & Online

Individual and couples counselling, faith-sensitive support, and Gottman-informed couples workshops — available in person in Edenvale and online.

Individual Counselling

Support for whatever you're carrying

A supportive, confidential space to work through anxiety, stress, grief, trauma, burnout, life changes and relationship difficulties — at a pace that feels manageable. Sessions are tailored to what you need, whether that's short-term help with something specific or longer-term support.

  • Anxiety
  • Stress
  • Grief and loss
  • Trauma
  • Burnout
  • Life transitions
  • Relationships

Couples Counselling

Reconnect and communicate more clearly

Practical, supportive counselling to help couples understand conflict patterns, communicate more clearly and rebuild connection. Whether you're navigating a specific challenge or a longer-standing pattern, counselling offers a calm space to work through it together.

  • Communication
  • Conflict patterns
  • Rebuilding connection
  • Trust
  • Life changes together

Christian Counselling

Faith-sensitive support, entirely optional

For clients who would like to include Christian beliefs and spiritual questions in the counselling process. This is entirely optional and offered only where it's welcome — counselling is always led by what matters to you.

In-person & online

Couples Workshops

Strengthen Your Relationship With Practical Tools

Couples workshops offer structured, practical guidance based on established relationship principles. They help couples better understand damaging patterns, improve communication and create healthier ways of working through challenges.

Available in selected Johannesburg locations and online.

What to Expect

Getting started is simple

Reaching out is often the hardest part. Here's what to expect from the first contact onward.

    Get in Touch

  2. 2

    Initial Conversation

    We discuss the support you're looking for and whether the practice is a suitable fit.

  3. 3

    Your First Session

    You begin exploring your concerns in a confidential and supportive environment.

  4. 4

    Ongoing Support

    We continue at an agreed pace, reviewing goals together as counselling progresses.
